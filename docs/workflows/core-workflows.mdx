---
title: "Core workflows"
description: "Move from local provider evidence to diagnosis, strategy, copy, and a launch decision."
---

SendLens combines read-only MCP tools with specialist skills and agents. The normal path is evidence first, recommendation second.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh the local workspace">
    Run `refresh_status` to see the current state, then `refresh_data` when a new provider pull is needed. A scoped campaign load can add deeper evidence without turning every question into a full refresh.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Triage what needs attention">
    Use `workspace_snapshot` and the workspace triage workflow to rank campaigns by bounce risk, lead runway, sender coverage, reply performance, sending state, and evidence gaps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare campaign evidence">
    Use `load_campaign_data` or `prepare_campaign_analysis` for the selected campaign. Fetch reply text only when the question requires it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diagnose before prescribing">
    The analyst workflow checks metrics, replies, ICP signals, copy, senders, and placement evidence while preserving exact, sampled, hybrid, reconstructed, and fetched labels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn findings into a campaign plan">
    The strategy workflow defines audience, offer, angle, sequence, and an experiment from validated evidence. Unsupported conclusions stay labeled as hypotheses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft and gate">
    The copy workflow produces evidence-backed variants. The launch workflow records blockers, measurement rules, and scale or stop criteria before execution happens in another system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start with plain-language questions

- “Which active campaign needs attention first, and why?”
- “What changed before reply rate dropped?”
- “Which sampled lead fields are associated with positive replies?”
- “Where does placement look healthy but the conversation still fail?”
- “Use the strongest validated evidence to recommend the next campaign test.”

## Read-only means no silent execution

SendLens can analyze provider evidence and recommend actions. It does not activate, pause, delete, or edit campaigns; change leads or accounts; send or reply to email; or manage provider webhooks and settings.

That separation lets operators use a provider control surface for execution and SendLens for local analysis, strategy, and launch gating.
